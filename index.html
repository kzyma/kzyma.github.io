Day1Sept 4

Naples → Monopoli

Land, Eat Something, Board the 3:15

Touch down at Naples Capodichino at 1PM. Alibus shuttle or taxi to Napoli Centrale (~20 min, €5 shuttle / €23 taxi fixed fare). Grab a sfogliatella at the station and board the 3:15PM Marino Autolinee coach from the Autostazione Metropark exit directly to Monopoli — roughly 4 hours, arriving early evening. Walk to the harbor, find dinner, decompress. The trip starts here.

Days2–5Sept 5–8

Monopoli, Puglia — 4 Nights

Adriatic Light & Old Town Lanes

Monopoli is the perfect entry point into Puglia — compact, beautiful, working harbor, whitewashed alleys, rocky coves. Rent a car here on Day 2 for the rest of the trip. Don't over-schedule: mornings at the harbor with espresso, afternoons at the beach or cycling the coast. Day trip south to Polignano a Mare (15 min by car) for the vertiginous cliff views and lunch above the Adriatic. Another day, head inland to Alberobello for the trulli landscape.

Pick up rental car Day 2 Day trip: Polignano a Mare Day trip: Alberobello Trulli Day trip: Castellana Grotte Harbor dinners

Day6Sept 9

Monopoli → Lecce

South to the Baroque Capital

Drive south from Monopoli along the SS16 or inland through the Valle d'Itria — about 1.5 hours to Lecce. Park at a garage near the old town (Parking Via Adua or Piazzale Merine are both close). Within five minutes of parking you're inside one of the most extraordinary baroque cityscapes in Europe. Check in, walk immediately to Piazza del Duomo as the afternoon light turns the golden Lecce stone amber.

Days7–8Sept 10–11

Lecce, Salento — 3 Nights

The Florence of the South

Lecce earns its nickname. Spend two days exploring: Basilica di Santa Croce, Piazza Sant'Oronzo with its Roman amphitheater, the winding backstreets. Pasticciotto from Caffè Alvino or Natale for breakfast every morning. Salento's Primitivo and Negroamaro wines are exceptional and cheap. Day trip to Otranto for its Byzantine mosaics and Aragonese castle, or Gallipoli for the cliff-top old town and beaches.

Basilica di Santa Croce Piazza del Duomo at dusk Day trip: Otranto Day trip: Gallipoli Pasticciotto: Caffè Alvino Negroamaro wine bars

Day9Sept 12

Lecce → Ostuni (Masseria)

Into the Olive Groves

Drive north ~1.5 hours from Lecce via the SS16. Stop in Ostuni old town for an hour — park at the base of the hill and walk up into the chalk-white labyrinth. Then head 5km south of Ostuni along the SS16 to Masseria Il Frantoio (address: SS16 Km 874). Check in, settle in, and have dinner on the property tonight — don't miss owner Armando's introduction to the estate.

Drive ~1.5 hrs from Lecce Stop: Ostuni White City Dinner at Frantoio tonight

Days10–11Sept 13–14

Masseria Il Frantoio, Ostuni — 3 Nights

Slow Days Among the Olives

The decompression stretch. Owner Armando personally leads a farm tour and narrates dinner every evening — don't skip it. The 16 rooms sit in 16th- and 19th-century stone farm buildings: terracotta floors, vaulted limestone ceilings, jasmine-scented courtyard. The pool is set among centuries-old olive trees. Spend at least one day not leaving. Day trips: Locorotondo and Cisternino (both 30 min) for the prettiest trulli-valley towns. Return the car from Ostuni for the Naples leg.

Armando's nightly estate dinner Pool among ancient olives Organic olive oil tasting Day trip: Locorotondo Day trip: Cisternino Breakfast included

Day13Sept 15–16

Naples, Campania — 2 Nights

Naples, Unfiltered

Walk Spaccanapoli, the straight ancient axis that cuts the city in two. Museo Nazionale Archeologico for the Pompeii artifacts. Morning trip to Pompeii or Herculaneum on the Circumvesuviana train. Sfogliatella from Attanasio for breakfast. Pizza at Sorbillo on Via dei Tribunali for dinner. Cocktails and nightlife in Chiaia afterward — that's where Naples hides its good bars.

Stop 3 · Ostuni — The Masseria Sept 11 – Sept 14 · 3 Nights

Two very different experiences — Borgo Egnazia is a full-scale world-class resort village, one of the most celebrated hotels in Italy. Masseria Il Frantoio is a working organic farm, intimate and owner-run. Both are in the Ostuni/Fasano corridor. The question is scale and vibe: resort luxury vs. authentic off-grid.

Our Pick · Off-Grid

Working Farm · 16 Rooms · Owner-Run

Masseria Il Frantoio

SS16 Km 874, Ostuni · 72-hectare olive estate

A working 72-hectare organic olive farm in 16th- and 19th-century stone buildings. Terracotta floors, limestone vaulted ceilings, jasmine courtyard, pool among ancient olives. Owner Armando leads a personal estate tour and narrates dinner from the farm's own produce every evening. The most authentically Pugliese experience available. Deeply quiet, deeply real. Book directly.

Getting Around

Transport,
Leg by Leg

Leg 1 · Sept 4

Naples → Monopoli

Catch the 3:15PM Marino Autolinee coach from the Autostazione Metropark exit at Napoli Centrale. Direct service to Monopoli, ~4 hours. Book at marinobus.it in advance — September afternoon buses fill. Tickets ~€17–45. Arrive Monopoli Via Lagravinese stop, short walk or taxi to accommodation.

🚌3:15PM Coach~4 hrs

Leg 2 · Sept 5 onward

Rental Car — Monopoli Base

Pick up a rental car in Monopoli on Day 2 (Sept 5). Use for all remaining travel: day trips from Monopoli, the drive to Lecce, Lecce day trips, drive to the Masseria, and the return to Naples. Europcar and Hertz have Monopoli locations — book in advance. Return the car either in Monopoli before the bus to Naples, or drop it in Naples directly.

🚗Rental CarSept 5–14/15

Leg 3 · Sept 9

Monopoli → Lecce

Drive south from Monopoli ~1.5 hours via SS16 coastal road or inland through the Valle d'Itria. Park in the old town area (Parking Via Adua). Car is useful for Lecce day trips to Otranto and Gallipoli. Keep it parked for the next 3 days otherwise — Lecce's centro storico is entirely walkable.

🚗Self-Drive~1.5 hrs

Leg 4 · Sept 12

Lecce → Masseria Il Frantoio, Ostuni

Drive north from Lecce ~1.5 hours via SS16. Stop in Ostuni old town for an hour on the way. Masseria is at SS16 Km 874, about 5km south of Ostuni. Car is useful for Valle d'Itria day trips from the Masseria (Locorotondo, Cisternino — both ~30 min).

🚗Self-Drive~1.5 hrs

Leg 5 · Sept 15 — Option A

Ostuni → Monopoli → Naples (Bus)

Drive from the Masseria back to Monopoli (~45 min), return the rental car there, then catch the direct Marino Autolinee coach back to Naples (~4 hrs). Cleaner car return, direct bus. Best if you want to avoid driving into Naples city traffic.

🚌Car + Bus~5 hrs total

Leg 5 · Sept 15 — Option B

Ostuni → Naples (Drive Direct)

Drive straight from the Masseria to Naples ~3.5 hours via A14/A3 motorway. Drop the car at a Naples rental depot (Capodichino Airport is easiest). Faster total journey, and you have the car for Amalfi or a final morning excursion if desired. Naples city driving is chaotic — factor that in.

🚗Drive Direct~3.5 hrs

Departure · Sept 16

Naples Hotel → Capodichino Airport

Capodichino (NAP) is 7km from central Naples. Official taxis have a fixed rate from the city center (~€23). Alibus shuttle from Piazza Garibaldi: €5, ~15 min. Book a taxi the night before — Naples traffic is unpredictable. Leave at least 90 min before departure.

✈️Taxi / Shuttle~20–30 min

Practical Notes

Things Worth
Knowing

🚌

The 3:15 Bus

Book the Marino Autolinee 3:15PM coach at marinobus.it as soon as flights are confirmed. September afternoon coaches from Naples to Puglia fill up. Have the booking on your phone. The Autostazione Metropark exit is the left side of Napoli Centrale heading toward the platforms.

🚗

Renting in Monopoli

Picking up the car in Monopoli on Day 2 keeps the first evening simple. Book with Europcar or Hertz — confirm they have a Monopoli location. Request a manual or automatic depending on preference (manuals are more common in Italy). A compact or mid-size handles the narrow roads well.

🌡️

September Weather

Early September in the deep south is warm — 28–32°C, some humidity. Softens mid-month. The Adriatic is perfect for swimming. Pack light linen. The masseria will be a cool stone sanctuary from the afternoon heat.

🏨

What to Book First

Masseria Il Frantoio (16 rooms) and Don Ferrante (10 rooms) fill fastest. Once accommodation decisions are made, book the 3:15PM bus, then the rental car. Lecce hotels are easier but Palazzo BN is also small — don't sit on it too long.

🫒

At the Masseria

Don't skip Armando's pre-dinner estate tour on the first night — it's the signature experience and sets the tone for the whole stay. Ask about the olive oil pressing schedule. The jasmine courtyard at aperitivo hour is not to be missed.

🍕

Naples Essentials

Sfogliatella from Attanasio (near Centrale) for breakfast. Pizza from Sorbillo on Via dei Tribunali — arrive at opening or queue. Negroni or Aperol in Chiaia afterward. For Pompeii, the Circumvesuviana from Piazza Garibaldi takes 40 min to Pompei Scavi.
