Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

Day 1 Sept 4

Naples → Monopoli

Land, Eat, Board a Bus South

You land at Naples Capodichino at 1PM. After clearing arrivals, take a taxi or Alibus shuttle to Napoli Centrale station (~20 min). From there, catch the direct Marino Autolinee or Itabus coach to Monopoli — departures throughout the afternoon, roughly 4 hours. You'll roll in as golden hour lights up the Adriatic. Drop bags, walk the old town harbor, find dinner.

Bus Direct ~4 hrs Book Marino or Itabus in advance

Days 2–5 Sept 5–8

Monopoli, Puglia

The Adriatic Gem

Monopoli is the entry point to Puglia's character — a compact medieval town on the Adriatic with a maze of whitewashed streets, a working fishing harbor, and beaches tucked into rocky coves. Four nights here gives you room to breathe. Day trips to Polignano a Mare (15 min south) for the vertigo-inducing coastal views, and Alberobello for the trulli. Have lunch at the harbor every day if you feel like it. Rent bikes for the coastal road. Don't plan too much.

Day Trip: Polignano a Mare Day Trip: Alberobello Trulli Day Trip: Castellana Grotte Harbor Dining

Day 6 Sept 9

Monopoli → Lecce

Head South to Baroque Country

Trenitalia Regionale from Monopoli to Lecce — about 1.5 hours, departures every hour or two. Arrive by late morning. Lecce is immediately legible: you step off the train and within 5 minutes you're in the middle of southern Italy's most extraordinary baroque cityscape. Check in, then get lost. Walk to Piazza del Duomo as dusk approaches — it may be the most beautiful square in Italy.

Train ~1.5 hrs Trenitalia Regionale

Days 7–8 Sept 10–11

Lecce, Salento

The Florence of the South

Lecce is the baroque capital of the south, built in warm golden sandstone called Lecce stone. Every church façade is a riot of carved angels and garlands. Walk it slowly — the Basilica di Santa Croce, Piazza Sant'Oronzo, the Roman amphitheater. Then escape the heat at a wine bar. Salento's wines (Primitivo, Negroamaro) are some of the best in Italy. Take an afternoon trip to Otranto for its Byzantine mosaics and cliff-side castle, or south to the tip of the heel at Santa Maria di Leuca.

Day 9 Sept 12

Lecce → Fasano / Ostuni Area

Into the Olive Groves

Pick up a rental car in Lecce for this leg — this is the move. Drive north through the Valle d'Itria, stopping at the whitewashed hill town of Ostuni (worth an hour to walk the old town and have espresso). Then arrive at your masseria between Fasano and Savelletri — the jewel of the trip. Settle in, do nothing. Have dinner on property.

Rent Car in Lecce Stop: Ostuni White City ~2 hr drive

Days 10–11 Sept 13–14

Masseria, Valle d'Itria

Slow Days at the Farmhouse

This is the decompression stretch. Pool, spa, estate-grown food. Wake up late. Do a cooking class. Take the shuttle to the private beach club. Explore the valley — Locorotondo, Cisternino, and Martina Franca are nearby. Return the car before heading to Naples. These two days are about letting Puglia fully land.

Day 12 Sept 15

Fasano → Naples

Return to the Volcano's Shadow

Return the rental car at Fasano train station (3km from the masseria). Board the Frecciargento or Intercity toward Naples — about 4.5 hours, often with one change at Bari. Arrive early evening. Drop bags at your hotel near the historic center. Go directly to dinner in the Quartieri Spagnoli — the narrow grid of streets where Naples is most itself. Find a pizza place with no English menu.

Day 13 Sept 15–16

Naples, Campania

Naples, Unfiltered

Two full evenings in Naples. It's a city you don't plan — you walk, get lost, eat, discover. Hit the Museo Nazionale Archeologico for the Pompeii artifacts (including the Secret Cabinet). Walk the Spaccanapoli axis. Take the Cumana or Circumvesuviana train out to Pompeii for a morning. Or just stay in the city and eat: sfogliatella for breakfast, panuozzo for lunch, pizza from Sorbillo or Starita for dinner. Negroni in the Chiaia neighborhood after.

Museo Nazionale Archeologico Day Trip: Pompeii Spaccanapoli Walk Pizza: Sorbillo or Starita Cocktails: Chiaia District

Where to Stay

Four Exceptional
Addresses

Stop 1 · Monopoli

Hotel Don Ferrante

Historic Fortress · 10 rooms · Dimore di Charme

A former clifftop fortress converted into 10 intimate rooms, each with stone walls, vaulted ceilings, and Adriatic views. The rooftop plunge pool and terrace dining are sensational. Locanda Don Ferrante restaurant downstairs is excellent. This is the most atmospheric and genuinely unique stay in Monopoli — stylish but never pretentious.

4 Nights · Sept 4–8

Stop 1 · Alternative · Monopoli

Il Melograno

16th-Century Masseria · Countryside · Family-Run

Puglia's original luxury masseria conversion — a 16th-century farmhouse surrounded by olive trees, 5km from Monopoli. Moorish courtyards, tiled rooms with fireplaces, an excellent spa, and a private beach club 7km away with shuttle service. If you prefer the countryside over the old town, this is the classic choice.

4 Nights · Sept 4–8

Stop 2 · Lecce

La Fiermontina Luxury Home

Palazzo · Historic Center · Design Hotel

A converted aristocratic palazzo in Lecce's historic center — rated among Italy's top boutique hotels. Sculpture garden, a beautiful pool, and rooms that feel like staying inside a contemporary art gallery set in a 17th-century shell. Walking distance to every sight in the city. The bar is exceptional.

3 Nights · Sept 8–11

Stop 2 · Alternative · Lecce

Risorgimento Resort

5-Star Resort · Old Town · Rooftop Terrace

Lecce's most celebrated full-service luxury hotel, set in a restored historic building right in the old town. Rooftop bar and restaurant with city views, spa, and genuinely impressive service. More resort-scaled than La Fiermontina — the right choice if you want comprehensive amenities alongside the baroque streets.

3 Nights · Sept 8–11

Stop 3 · Fasano · The Highlight

Masseria Torre Coccaro

16th-Century Fortified Farm · MICHELIN Key · Beach Club

The crown jewel of Pugliese masseria hotels. A 16th-century fortified watchtower turned luxury family-run estate, surrounded by centuries-old olive groves near the Adriatic coast. Aveda spa in natural caves, private beach club with shuttle, farm-to-table restaurant, cooking school, wine tastings, and a 9-hole golf course on site. One MICHELIN Key. Book the Savelletri exit off E55.

3 Nights · Sept 11–14

Stop 4 · Naples

Palazzo Alabardieri

Historic Palazzo · Chiaia District · Boutique

A romantically restored palazzo in the upscale Chiaia neighborhood — quieter and more elegant than the chaotic centro storico, but walking distance to everything. Rooftop terrace breakfast, classic Neapolitan décor, and impeccable service. Perfect as a civilized final two nights before the flight home. The nightlife and restaurants of Chiaia are right outside.

2 Nights · Sept 14–16

Getting Around

Transport Leg by Leg

Leg 1

Naples → Monopoli

Direct coach from Napoli Centrale (Autostazione Metropark exit, near the station). Marino Autolinee, Itabus, or FlixBus — 3–4 departures per afternoon. Journey ~4 hrs. Book at least a week ahead; tickets €17–45. Arrive at Monopoli Via Nicola Lagravinese stop, short taxi or walk to hotel.
🚌 Direct Bus
~4 hrs

Leg 2

Monopoli → Lecce

Trenitalia Regionale from Monopoli station, heading south on the Adriatica coastal line. Departures roughly every 1–2 hours. About 1.5 hours direct. Tickets ~€12–18. Book on Trenitalia.com. Arrive Lecce Centrale — most hotels are a short walk or taxi from the station.
🚆 Regional Train
~1.5 hrs

Leg 3

Lecce → Fasano (Masseria)

Pick up a rental car in Lecce — Europcar and Hertz have locations near the train station. Drive north via SS16 or the A14 motorway, stopping in Ostuni (~1.5 hrs). Then follow signs to Savelletri and Masseria Torre Coccaro off the E55 Savelletri exit. Total drive ~2 hrs. Drop the car at Fasano train station (3km from masseria) before your Naples train.
🚗 Rental Car
~2 hrs

Leg 4

Fasano → Naples

From Fasano station (3km from masseria — arrange hotel taxi), take Trenitalia toward Bari Centrale then connect to a Frecciargento or Intercity toward Naples. Total journey ~4.5–5 hrs. Book on Trenitalia.com in advance, especially for the high-speed connection. Arrive Napoli Centrale.
🚆 Train via Bari
~4.5 hrs

Departure Day

Naples Hotel → Capodichino Airport

Naples Capodichino (NAP) is only 7km from the city center. Taxi from Chiaia or centro storico takes 20–30 minutes. Official taxis have a fixed rate from the city center (~€23 to the airport). Also the Alibus shuttle from Piazza Garibaldi takes 15 min for €5. Book taxis the night before and allow plenty of time — Naples traffic is real.

Things to Know

Travel Notes for the South

🌡️

Weather in Late September

Southern Italy in early September is still very warm — expect 28–32°C (82–90°F) with high humidity. It cools slightly mid-September. The Adriatic is warm enough to swim. Light clothing, linen if possible. The masseria will be a cool stone sanctuary.

🍽️

Eating in Puglia

Puglia's food is earthy and ingredient-led: orecchiette with broccoli rabe, burrata made fresh that morning, grilled octopus, fava bean puree with chicory, taralli. In Lecce, get a pasticciotto at Natale bakery for breakfast. Dinner reservations at popular spots are essential in September.

🚌

Booking Transport

Book your Marino Autolinee or Itabus Naples–Monopoli coach as soon as your flights are set — afternoon departures fill up. Trenitalia tickets for regional trains can usually be bought day-of but intercity connections should be reserved. The rental car leg just needs a Lecce pickup booked in advance.

🏨

Booking Hotels

Don Ferrante has only 10 rooms — book immediately. Masseria Torre Coccaro and La Fiermontina should also be prioritized. September is still high season for Italian travel. Book all accommodations directly if possible; many boutique properties give better rates and perks than OTAs.

💳

Money & Practical Stuff

Card payment is accepted almost everywhere now in southern Italy, but carry €50–100 cash for small towns, bars, and market stalls. Download the Trenitalia app. Get a local SIM or international plan — maps and translation are essential. Pharmacies (green cross signs) are everywhere and excellent for minor issues.

🌊

Beach Strategy

Puglia's best swimming is from rocky coves near Polignano a Mare, from the Torre Coccaro beach club, and from Otranto's clear water. September means the August crowds have thinned — you'll find space. Most masseria beach clubs require booking shuttles in advance; the hotel will arrange it.
