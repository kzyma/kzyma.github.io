Eat Everything

The Dishes to Know

🍝
Orecchiette
Little ear-shaped pasta, hand-rolled by Bari nonnas. With cime di rapa or ragù. The soul of Puglia.
🧀
Burrata
Invented in Puglia. Eaten fresh within hours of being made. Nothing elsewhere compares — order it constantly.
🥙
Panzerotto
Bari's fried street food icon. Dough pockets stuffed with tomato and mozzarella. Eat one standing at the stall.
🐙
Polpo & Ricci
Grilled octopus and raw sea urchin. Order both at every harbor and beach club you visit.
🫘
Fave e Cicoria
Fava bean purée with sautéed chicory. The peasant soul food of Puglia — surprisingly extraordinary.
🧁
Pasticciotto
Lecce's custard-filled shortcrust pastry, eaten warm at breakfast with espresso. Invented here. Non-negotiable.
🌿
Taralli
Olive oil and wine biscuit rings — snack on them constantly. Buy several bags to bring home.
🍷
Primitivo & Verdeca
The regional reds (big, dark, extraordinary) and whites (crisp, mineral). Order local wine everywhere.
Curated Dining

Restaurant Guide

Highly-rated, locally-rooted picks for each base. Book dinner ahead wherever indicated — late September is still busy at the best spots.

Polignano a Mare
⭐ Book Way Ahead
Grotta Palazzese
★★★★ (4.0 · 4,000+ reviews)
Cave dining built into the cliff face over the Adriatic — one of the most theatrical settings in Italy. The experience is the point. Budget ~€150–200pp with wine. Book 4–6 weeks ahead. The 7:30pm seating gives you sunset into candlelit dark.
Order: Tasting menu with wine pairing
Local Favorite
Antiche Mura
★★★★☆ (4.6 · 2,300+ reviews)
Warm, elegant atmosphere in the centro storico. Superb tasting menu with fresh Pugliese seafood. Excellent service, great value. The sporcamuss dessert (puff pastry with custard) is a must-order.
Order: Seafood tasting menu · Orecchiette · Sporcamuss
Seafront Lunch
Calapà
★★★★★ (4.7 · 345 reviews)
Excellent seafood on the lungomare, relaxed atmosphere, staff who debone your whole fish tableside. Squid ink pasta and grilled orata are outstanding. Perfect for a long relaxed lunch after the morning beach.
Order: Squid ink pasta · Whole grilled fish · Tuna tartare
Dinner Alternative
La Locanda Porta Picc
★★★★☆ (4.6 · 2,290 reviews)
Catch-of-the-day seafood presented tableside. Excellent risotto, solid wine list, beautiful ambiance. Good backup if Grotta Palazzese is fully booked — equally special in a different way.
Order: Catch of the day · Potato-crusted sea bass
Gelato Stop
Gelateria Calore
★★★★★ (Local institution)
Polignano is famous for gelato made from regional almonds and local figs. This is one of the original spots. Go after the morning beach or before the evening stroll — do it at least twice.
Order: Almond · Figs & Walnuts · Pistachio
Valle d'Itria
⭐ Home Restaurant
Terra Mia — Chef Pasquale
★★★★★ (5.0 · 47 reviews)
A home restaurant in Martina Franca — Pasquale cooks wave after wave of dishes rooted in 100-year-old farmers' recipes: antipasti, handmade orecchiette, bombette (Pugliese stuffed pork rolls). Guests call it "what you dream of when you book Italy." €30pp, book ahead.
Order: Let Pasquale decide — he'll keep bringing food until you stop him
⭐ Most Reviews
La Pasteria — Martina Franca
★★★★★ (4.9 · 7,200+ reviews)
One of the highest-rated restaurants in all of Puglia with an extraordinary volume of reviews. Homemade pasta, outstanding tiramisu, impeccable service. Fills up fast even on weekdays — book ahead. Guests call it "the best of the entire trip" consistently.
Order: Fresh pasta of the day · Lasagna · House tiramisu
Alberobello
Casa Amatulli
★★★★☆ (4.7 · 1,600+ reviews)
Family-run trattoria in the heart of Alberobello. Authentic, generous, joyful. Complimentary pizza and olives to start, homemade pasta and excellent local wine. Multiple rooms so it doesn't feel crowded. A reliable and genuinely warm choice.

Trulli e Puglia Wine Bar

Subterranean wine bar in Alberobello — best spot in the valley for regional wine, cold cuts, and local cheese. Host Mimmo is exceptional. Perfect long lazy afternoon pit stop between the hilltop towns.

Ostuni Pick

★★★★☆ (4.4 · 480 reviews)
Michelin-recommended gem in Ostuni's old city. Husband-and-wife team: she cooks, he runs the floor. Five-course set menu for €35 — guests call it "the best meal in all of Italy." Very small; book well ahead for dinner. Worth the planning.

Good to Know

Practical Notes

🚗
Getting Around
  • Pick up rental car at Bari Airport on Day 1
  • Car essential for Itria Valley — masserias and towns require it
  • Polignano: paid lots outside the old town, walk in — don't drive the centro storico
  • Lecce ZTL zones — confirm hotel parking before driving in
  • Return car at Bari Airport on Day 13 before departure
🗓️
Late September Timing
  • August crowds are completely gone — significantly better
  • Sea temperature 26–28°C — full swimming season
  • Daytime 26–28°C, evenings cooling to 18–20°C — ideal
  • Harvest season: olive oil, wine, figs, grapes at peak quality
  • Some beach clubs scale back hours — book coastal lunch ahead
📋
Booking Priorities
  • Grotta Palazzese — 4–6 weeks ahead minimum
  • Tipico Restaurant, Lecce — small; book ahead
  • Osteria Piazzetta Cattedrale, Ostuni — tiny; book ahead
  • Terra Mia & La Pasteria, Martina Franca — book ahead
  • Masseria private dinner — arrange with host at check-in
  • Boat tour (sea grottos) — book at harbor day-of or night before
🍽️
Italian Dining Rhythm
  • Lunch: 12:30–2:30pm. Dinner: 8–10pm. Don't arrive at 6pm.
  • Aperitivo hour: 6–8pm — Campari, Aperol, or local amaro
  • Coperto (cover charge) is standard and not optional
  • Digestivo often complimentary at good restaurants
  • Standing espresso at the bar is cheaper and tastes better
🗣️
Language Notes
  • English understood in tourist-facing areas; less so in rural Itria Valley
  • Google Translate camera mode works excellently for menus
  • Key phrases: buongiorno, grazie, prego, per favore, il conto
  • A few Italian words go a very long way — locals respond warmly
💶
Money & Budget
  • Cards accepted widely at hotels and most restaurants
  • Cash needed for street food, small trattorias, and markets
  • €20–30 per person per day in small bills for incidentals
  • Tipping not required; €2–5 rounding up is appreciated
  • Good local wine with dinner: €20–40 per bottle
