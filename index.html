Where You're Going

The Destinations

Three very different places, each with its own character — a harbour city, a Baroque jewel, and an ancient working farm.

4 nights · Sept 4–8
Monopoli
Puglia · Adriatic Coast

A fishing port turned slow-travel favourite, with a walled old city right on the sea. The harbour is lined with trabucchi (ancient fishing machines), the beaches are a short drive away, and the evening passeggiata is genuinely charming. Base for day trips to Alberobello and Polignano a Mare.

Our townhome ↗
4 nights · Sept 8–12
Lecce
Puglia · The Baroque Capital

Often called the "Florence of the South," Lecce is dripping in ornate Baroque architecture carved from soft golden sandstone. The centro storico is traffic-free and walkable; the food scene punches well above its size. Spend hours just wandering.

Our hotel ↗
3 nights · Sept 12–15
Ostuni / Masseria
Puglia · Valle d'Itria

The "White City" on its hill, surrounded by a sea of ancient olive trees that seem to go on forever. Our base, Masseria Il Frantoio, is a 16th-century working farm where dinner is a communal event and the pace is that of the harvest. One of the most beautiful places in Italy.

Eat · Drink · Linger

Food & Drink

Puglia is one of Italy's great food regions — orecchiette, burrata, fava bean purée, sea urchin, local Primitivo wine. Here's where to eat and drink at each stop.

Naples
L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele

The most famous pizza in the world. Only two kinds: marinara and margherita. Queue early or book ahead. This is a pilgrimage.

🍕 Iconic · Cash only
Naples
Trattoria da Nennella

A wonderfully chaotic Neapolitan trattoria in the Quartieri Spagnoli. Cheap, generous, and loud. The locals eat here; so should you.

🍝 Local favourite
Monopoli
Osteria Il Guazzetto

Right in the old city, this intimate osteria does beautiful raw seafood and fresh pasta with local catches. The sea urchin spaghetti is extraordinary.

🦔 Seafood · Book ahead
Monopoli
Cantine Albea

A local winery with a restaurant attached — sit among the vines and work through the Primitivo di Manduria alongside rustic Pugliese dishes. Excellent value.

🍷 Wine country
Polignano a Mare
Grotta Palazzese

Dinner in a cave carved into the cliff above the Adriatic. Touristy and expensive but jaw-dropping — splurge on one big dinner here if the group is up for it.

🌊 Special occasion
Lecce
Alle Due Corti

The most beloved traditional restaurant in Lecce. Classics like orecchiette al ragù, ciceri e tria (pasta with chickpeas), and braised horse meat cooked the old way. Reserve well in advance.

🍴 Traditional · Essential
Lecce
Craft Beer & Cocktails at Piazza Sant'Oronzo

The main piazza comes alive after sunset. Pull up chairs at any of the bars ringing the Roman amphitheatre ruins, order Aperol spritzes, and watch Lecce's beautiful nightly passeggiata unfold.

🍸 Evening ritual
Lecce
Pasticceria Natale

Start every morning here with a pasticciotto — Lecce's own pastry, a short-crust shell filled with custard. It is one of the best things you will eat on this trip, guaranteed.

☕ Breakfast · Must-do
Ostuni
Masseria Il Frantoio (dinner)

Dinner is included at the masseria and is not to be missed — a long communal table, antipasti from the garden, pasta made that afternoon, local wine, and dessert under the stars. The highlight of the whole trip.

🌿 Included · Unmissable
Ostuni
Osteria del Tempo Perso

Tucked into a grotto in Ostuni's white hilltop town, this is romantic, candlelit dining done beautifully — foraged mushrooms, burrata, and aged local cheeses.

Adventures & Diversions

Things To Do

Day trips, beaches, history, wandering — curated for four people who love doing it all.

🏛
Naples · Day 1
National Archaeological Museum

Home to the world's finest collection of Roman art from Pompeii and Herculaneum — the Alexander Mosaic alone is worth the visit. Allow 2–3 hours.

🌊
Near Monopoli
Polignano a Mare

A 20-min drive north brings you to Puglia's most photogenic town — a medieval village perched on limestone cliffs above crystal-clear sea. Swim in the sea cave, walk the old town, eat gelato.

🏡
Near Monopoli
Trulli of Alberobello

A UNESCO World Heritage Site — hundreds of conical stone huts called trulli, unique in the world and utterly surreal in person. About 45 minutes from Monopoli. Do the morning; avoid midday crowds.

🏊
Monopoli Area
Spiaggia di Porto Giardino

One of the most beautiful beach spots near Monopoli — a sheltered cove with turquoise Adriatic water and a beach club for sun loungers, drinks, and doing absolutely nothing. September is peak weather.

🥂
Itria Valley
Wine Tasting in the Itria Valley

The Valle d'Itria between Monopoli and Lecce is carpeted in ancient vineyards. Several masserie offer tastings of Primitivo, Negroamaro, and Verdeca — book an afternoon session at a winery like Masseria Il Frantoio or Tenuta Viglione.

🧭
Lecce
Baroque Walking Tour

Hire a local guide for a 2-hour morning walk through Lecce's old city. The Piazza del Duomo, Basilica di Santa Croce, and the Roman amphitheatre ruins in the middle of town are staggering. The stone glows at golden hour.

🏄
Near Lecce
Torre dell'Orso & the Sea

A 30-min drive east of Lecce reaches the Salento coast — Torre dell'Orso is one of Puglia's finest beaches, with clear shallow water and a gentle vibe. Great for a beach day when the sightseeing legs need a rest.

🏘
Near Ostuni
Locorotondo & Cisternino

Two perfectly preserved hilltop white villages within 30 minutes of the masseria. Locorotondo has a rooftop terrace with views across the valley; Cisternino is famous for its traditional bombette (meat rolls cooked over open fire). Evening aperitivo material.

🫒
Masseria Il Frantoio
Olive Oil Experience

The masseria offers tours of the ancient frantoio (olive press) and the working groves. September is early harvest — you may be able to participate. Stock up on their house-pressed oil before leaving.

🎵
Ostuni / Lecce
Pizzica & Live Folk Music

Puglia has its own ancient folk music tradition — the pizzica, descended from the tarantella. September sometimes sees late-summer festivals. Ask locally about piazzas with live music; spontaneous sessions happen in both Ostuni and Lecce's old towns.

🛍
Lecce & Ostuni
Ceramics & Local Markets

Puglia has a strong ceramics tradition — both Lecce and Ostuni have artisan workshops selling beautifully painted ware. Lecce also has a Saturday market with local produce, cheese, and cured meats that makes for an excellent breakfast provision run.

🌅
Any Stop
The Passeggiata

Every Italian town does the evening passeggiata — a slow walk through the main street around 7–9pm as everyone comes out to see and be seen. Join it everywhere. Buy a gelato, find a bench, and just watch. This is the heartbeat of southern Italy.

Good to Know

Tips & Essentials

A few practical and cultural notes to make everything smoother.

🚗
Hire Car

Pick up the hire car in Monopoli when heading to Lecce. Having a car for the Lecce and Ostuni legs is essential for day trips and getting to the masseria. GPS is fine; many back roads aren't on Google Maps — follow signs.

💶
Cash is King

Smaller trattorias, market stalls, and bars often don't take cards. Withdraw cash at a bank ATM on arrival and keep €100–200 on hand at all times. Avoid airport exchange booths.

🕑
Southern Italian Time

Lunch runs 1–3pm, dinner starts late (8:30–9:30pm), and the afternoon riposo (roughly 1–4pm) means many shops and sites close mid-day. Embrace it — it's the right way to live in 35°C heat.

🌡
September Weather

Expect 28–32°C, low humidity, and practically zero rain. The sea is at its warmest (24–26°C). Pack light linen, sunscreen, and a layer for evenings. Sandals all day. It will be glorious.

🗣
Language

English is less prevalent here than in northern Italy. Learn a few basics: buongiorno, grazie, prego, il conto per favore (the bill please), and posso avere (can I have). Even a clumsy attempt is warmly received.

📱
Data & SIMs

Buy a local SIM (TIM or Iliad) at the Naples airport for cheap data. Alternatively, check your phone plan's EU roaming — most US carriers include it at no extra cost. Download Google Maps offline for Puglia before you fly.
