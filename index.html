Our Route

The Journey

Flying in and out of Bari. We arc south through cliff-coast Polignano, into the trulli countryside of Itria Valley, then deep south to baroque Lecce and the crystal Salento coast, before finishing one final night back in Bari.

Four Chapters

Where We Stay

Each base is completely different from the last — cliff-coast drama, rural trulli farmhouse, full baroque city, and gritty port capital. No two nights feel the same.

Eat Everything

The Dishes You Must Try

🍝
Orecchiette
Little ear-shaped pasta, made by hand by Bari nonnas. With cime di rapa (bitter greens) or tomato ragù.
🧀
Burrata
Invented in Puglia. Eaten fresh, within hours of being made. Nothing elsewhere compares.
🥙
Panzerotto
Bari's fried street food icon. Dough pockets stuffed with tomato and mozzarella, eaten standing at the street stall.
🐙
Polpo & Ricci
Grilled octopus and raw sea urchin. Order both at every harbor or beach club you eat at.
🫘
Fave e Cicoria
Fava bean purée with sautéed chicory. The peasant soul food of Puglia — surprisingly extraordinary.
🧁
Pasticciotto
Lecce's custard-filled shortcrust pastry, eaten warm for breakfast with a caffè. Invented here. Do not skip.
🌿
Taralli
Crunchy ring-shaped savory biscuits made with olive oil and wine. Snack on them constantly. Buy bags to take home.
🍷
Primitivo & Verdeca
The regional reds (Primitivo, big and dark) and whites (Verdeca, crisp and mineral) — order local wine everywhere.
Curated Dining

Restaurant Shortlist

Highly-rated, locally-rooted picks for each base. Booking ahead is recommended for dinner — especially in Polignano and Lecce in late September.

Polignano a Mare
⭐ Book Ahead
Grotta Palazzese
★★★★★ (Iconic)
Cave dining built into the cliff face over the sea. One of the most theatrical dining settings in Italy. The food matches the setting. Book several weeks ahead — tables fill fast.
Order: The tasting menu — let the chef decide
Local Favorite
Antiche Mura
★★★★☆ (4.6 · 2,300+ reviews)
Warm, elegant atmosphere in the centro storico with a superb tasting menu. Fresh seafood served the Pugliese way — clean, simple, and outstanding. Excellent service. Very good value for the quality.
Order: Seafood tasting menu · Local orecchiette · Sporcamuss dessert
Seafront Pick
Calapà
★★★★★ (4.7 · 345 reviews)
Excellent seafood restaurant on the lungomare with a relaxed atmosphere and impeccable fish. Staff help debone your whole fish at the table. The squid ink pasta and grilled orata are exceptional. Great for a relaxed lunch.
Order: Squid ink pasta · Whole grilled fish · Tuna tartare
Valle d'Itria
⭐ Must-Eat
Casa Amatulli
★★★★☆ (4.7 · 1,600+ reviews)
Family-run trattoria in Alberobello — authentic, generous, joyful. Multiple dining rooms so it doesn't feel crowded. Homemade pasta, excellent local wine, and complimentary pizza and olives to start. Reviewers call it a "must-eat."
Order: Homemade pasta · Prosciutto e formaggio platter
Wine & Cheese
Trulli e Puglia Wine Bar
★★★★★ (4.8 · 430 reviews)
A subterranean oasis in Alberobello — the best place in the valley to drink wine and eat regional cold cuts and cheese. Host Mimmo is exceptional. The perfect after-exploring pit stop. Great for a long lazy afternoon.
Order: Cheese tasting board · Assorted meats · Local Primitivo
Trulli Terrace
Corte dei Trulli
★★★★☆ (4.5 · 490 reviews)
Beautiful upstairs terrace dining in the heart of Alberobello. Warm, charming atmosphere with authentic Pugliese food — burrata, superb steak, traditional pasta. Better than the more touristy spots on the main strip.
Order: Burrata · Bistecca · House pasta
Lecce
⭐ Book Ahead
Tipico Restaurant
★★★★★ (4.9 · 114 reviews)
Reviewers call it the best restaurant experience of their entire Italian trip. Housed in a vaulted former bakery of a cloister. Chef-owner Gianna is extraordinary — warm, passionate, technically brilliant. "Burnt" spaghetti and parmigiana are standouts. People break their "no-repeat" rule for this place.
Order: Charred spaghetti · Parmigiana · Tiramisu
Lunch Staple
Taula
★★★★★ (4.8 · 1,195 reviews)
Lively, buzzy Mediterranean restaurant in the city center — always packed, fast service, and genuinely delicious. Known for puccia (local stuffed bread) and perfectly cooked octopus. Very well priced for the quality and location. Great for an animated lunch.
Order: Octopus with mashed potato · Puccia con mortadella e burrata
Evening Option
La Locanda Porta Picc
★★★★☆ (4.6 · 2,290 reviews)
Well-loved restaurant in Polignano that also has a Lecce following — worth seeking out for an elegant dinner. Fresh fish shown tableside before ordering. Excellent risotto, beautiful wine list. Good for a special evening.
Order: Catch of the day · Sea bass crust · Wine pairing
Bari
⭐ The One
La Cantina dello Zio
★★★★☆ (4.7 · 15,400+ reviews)
One of the most beloved restaurants in Bari Vecchia — feels like "having dinner with family." Complimentary olives, taralli, and bruschetta to start; limoncello to finish. Orecchiette, gnocchi, and pizza all exceptional. Host Anna is legendary. The warmth here is real.
Order: Orecchiette · Pizza · House gnocchi · Limoncello
Old Town Gem
Le Nicchie
★★★★☆ (4.7 · 3,100+ reviews)
Charming outdoor terrace in a picturesque old-town street. Consistently excellent seafood pasta and the local specialty spaghetti all'assassina (spicy, caramelized). One of the best in Bari for traditional Pugliese cooking. Good house wine.
Order: Spaghetti all'assassina · Seafood pasta · Octopus
Street Food
Bari Vecchia Street Circuit
★★★★★ (Free)
Not a restaurant — a circuit. Start with raw sea urchin at the harbor fish stalls. Then panzerotto fritto from Panificio Fiore on Via Chirulli. End with fresh taralli from any of the bread bakeries in the old town. This is the essential Bari food experience.
Order: Ricci di mare · Panzerotto · Taralli caldi

Good to Know

Practical Notes

🚗
Getting Around
  • Pick up rental car at Bari Airport on Day 1
  • Car essential for Itria Valley — the masserie and towns require it
  • Polignano parking is limited; use the paid lots outside and walk in
  • Lecce has ZTL zones — confirm hotel parking before driving in
  • Return car at Bari Airport on Day 13 before departure
🗓️
Late September Timing
  • Peak summer crowds (August) are gone — significantly better
  • Sea temperature still 26–28°C — full swimming season
  • Daytime 26–28°C, evenings cooling to 18–20°C — perfect
  • Harvest season: olive oil, wine, local produce at peak quality
  • Some beach clubs begin scaling back — book lunch ahead
📋
Booking Priorities
  • Grotta Palazzese, Polignano — book weeks ahead
  • Tipico Restaurant, Lecce — book ahead; small and very popular
  • Masseria private dinner — arrange with host at check-in
  • Boat tour (sea grottos) — book at harbor morning of, or day before
  • La Fiermontina, Lecce — high demand; book early
🍽️
Italian Dining Rhythm
  • Lunch: 12:30–2:30pm. Dinner: 8–10pm. Don't arrive at 6pm.
  • Aperitivo hour typically 6–8pm — order Campari, Aperol, or local amaro
  • Coperto (cover charge) is standard — not optional, not a scam
  • Digestivo (amaro, limoncello) often complimentary at good restaurants
  • Standing espresso at the bar costs less and tastes better
🗣️
Language
  • English widely understood in tourist areas; less so in rural Itria Valley
  • A few Italian phrases go a long way — locals respond warmly
  • Google Translate camera mode works well for menus
  • Key phrases: grazie, prego, per favore, il conto (the bill), buongiorno
  • Pugliese dialect is its own thing — don't worry about it
💶
Money & Budget
  • Cards widely accepted in hotels and most restaurants
  • Cash needed for smaller trattorias, street food, and markets
  • Bring €20–30 in small bills per person per day for incidentals
  • Tipping not required; rounding up or leaving €2–5 is appreciated
  • Wine with dinner typically €20–40 for a good local bottle
