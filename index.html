Day by Day

The Full Plan

A mix of highlights, open relaxation time, beach days, and city energy — never over-scheduled.

```
I
Polignano a Mare
4 Nights · Days 1–4 · Adriatic Cliff Coast
1
Arrival
Landing & First Wander
No Agenda · Settle In
✈️Fly into Bari, pick up the rental car, 35-min drive south to Polignano
🏨Check in, drop bags — the afternoon is yours
🚶Slow wander through the white centro storico, out to the cliff overlook at Lama Monachile for your first Adriatic sunset
🍽️No fixed dinner — explore the old town alleys and duck into wherever looks right. Every street has something good.
💡 Arrival day is decompression. The town is the activity — let it unfold on its own terms.
2
Beach
Sea Grottos & Grotta Palazzese
Boat Tour · Cliff Coves · Cave Dining
☀️Morning at Lama Monachile — the iconic pebble beach under the bridge. Arrive by 9am to beat the crowds.
🚤Afternoon boat tour of the sea grottos (Grotta della Poesia, Grotta di Colombo) — ~1.5 hrs, book at the harbor
🍦Gelato at Gelateria Calore — almond, fig, pistachio. Polignano is legendary for it.
🕯️Dinner at Grotta Palazzese — cave dining built into the sea cliff. One of the most theatrical settings in Italy. Book weeks ahead, aim for 7:30pm for the sunset-to-candlelit transition.
💡 Book Grotta Palazzese well before the trip — it fills fast. 7:30pm catches last light over the sea.
3
Day Trip
Monopoli & Coastal Countryside
Walled Port Town · Olive Groves · 15–30 min Drive
🏰Morning in Monopoli — a beautiful walled port town 10km north; Castello Carlo V on the waterfront, the fishing harbor, lively market streets
🍝Lunch in Monopoli's old town — excellent local trattorias along the harbor (see restaurant guide)
🌿Afternoon drive through Fasano's olive grove countryside — the landscape shifts toward Valle d'Itria
🎵Evening back in Polignano — late September often has live music in the cliff bars and piazzas
4
Slow Day
Market Morning & Final Cove
Relax · Last Adriatic Swim · Farewell Aperitivo
☕Slow morning — espresso and cornetto at a bar, read on a terrace above the sea
🧀Local food market and alimentari: pick up fresh burrata, taralli, and local olive oil
🏊Afternoon swim at one of the smaller cliff coves accessible by stone stair — quieter than Lama Monachile
🥂Farewell aperitivo at sunset — Pugliese amaro or limoncello before heading inland tomorrow
II
Valle d'Itria
3 Nights · Days 5–7 · Masseria Farmhouse
5
Arrive
Into the Countryside + Alberobello
Masseria Arrival · UNESCO Trulli Village
🚗Check out of Polignano, ~45-min drive inland through olive groves and dry-stone walls to the masseria
🏡Arrive at the farmhouse — explore the property, pool, orchards, countryside. Let it settle.
🍄Late afternoon in Alberobello (20–30 min) — best after 5pm when day-trippers leave and the cone-roofed lanes go quiet and golden
🍽️Dinner in Alberobello or nearby — try braised lamb or fave e cicoria (fava bean purée with bitter greens)
💡 Alberobello is more impressive in person than in photos. Linger past 5pm — the atmosphere shifts completely.
6
Explore
Ostuni & Locorotondo Wine Country
White City · DOC Vineyards · Masseria Pool Evening
🤍Morning in Ostuni — the brilliant white hilltop city. Cathedral, labyrinthine medieval center, outer wall panoramas across olive groves to the sea
🍷Afternoon: Locorotondo DOC wine country — crisp Verdeca whites; stop at a cantina for a tasting
🏘️Wander Locorotondo itself — circular hilltop town with flower-lined alleys, one of the most photogenic in Puglia
🏊Evening back at the masseria pool with a glass of local white
7
Finale
Martina Franca & Masseria Dinner
Music Town · Artisan Shops · Private Farm Dinner
🎼Morning in Martina Franca — the most urban town in the valley; baroque Piazza Roma, excellent coffee bars, ceramics and textile artisan workshops
🛍️Browse the artisan shops — Puglia hand-painted pottery travels well and makes great gifts
🌾Afternoon: get fully lost on the country roads between masserias — no agenda, stop when something looks beautiful
🍖Private masseria dinner using the farm's own produce and wine — arrange this at check-in. Often the best meal of the entire trip.
💡 Ask the masseria host to arrange a private farm dinner with local wine. This is the unmissable Puglia experience.

Lecce & Salento

8
Arrive
Into the Baroque Capital
Lecce Centro Storico · First Impressions
🚗Drive ~1.5 hrs south from the masseria to Lecce
⛪Baroque circuit: Piazza del Duomo, Santa Croce Basilica (the city's masterpiece), Roman Amphitheatre in Piazza Sant'Oronzo
🎨Pop into a contemporary gallery — Lecce has a genuine local art scene woven through the historic palazzi

9
Beach
Salento Coast Beach Day
Ionian or Adriatic · 30–50 min Drive
🏖️Ionian side: Porto Cesareo or Torre dell'Orso for crystalline turquoise; Adriatic side: Baia dei Turchi near Otranto — the most beautiful beach in Salento, protected nature reserve
🐠Full beach day — late September water is 26–28°C and August crowds are completely gone
🦞Lunch at a masseria or beach club: raw sea urchin, grilled octopus, fried anchovies
🌆Evening in Lecce's side streets — local, non-touristy energy off the main drag
💡 Baia dei Turchi: 20-min walk through pine forest. Get there before 10am — completely worth it.
10
Day Trip
Otranto & Gallipoli
Walled Coastal Towns · Full Day Out
🏰Morning in Otranto (45 min east) — Norman castle, UNESCO cathedral with a 12th-century mosaic floor covering the entire nave, Venetian walls over electric-blue water
🌊Quick swim at the cliffs near Otranto
🎭Afternoon in Gallipoli (1 hr west, Ionian side) — baroque island city on a bridge, fish market, beautiful harbor, completely different energy
🍽️Dinner in Gallipoli at the port before driving back
11
City Day
Art, Shops & Lecce Nightlife
No Driving · Full City Day · Late Night
🖼️Museo Faggiano — a family excavated their basement and found 2,000 years of layered history. One of the most unique museums in Italy.
🧁Pasticciotto at Pasticceria Ascalone — warm, with a caffè. The original Lecce custard pastry, invented here in 1745.
🛍️Boutiques and ceramics workshops along Via Trinchese and Via Paladini
🎵Aperitivo and nightlife around Piazzetta Vittorio Emanuele II — the real Lecce going-out scene
🍸Late cocktails at a rooftop or courtyard bar in one of the baroque palazzi
💡 Lecce nightlife peaks around 9pm — embrace the rhythm entirely.
```

Eat Everything
The Dishes You Must Try
🍝
Orecchiette
Puglia's signature pasta — ear-shaped, hand-rolled by Bari nonnas. With cime di rapa or tomato ragù. Watch them make it live in Bari Vecchia.
🧀
Burrata
Invented in Puglia. Eaten within hours of being made. Nothing anywhere else compares. Order it at every table.
🥙
Panzerotto
Bari's iconic fried street food — dough pockets stuffed with tomato and mozzarella. Eaten standing outside the bakery, hot and messy.
🐙
Polpo & Ricci
Grilled octopus and raw sea urchin. Order both at every harbor or beach club. The ricci fresh from Salento are extraordinary.
🫘
Fave e Cicoria
Fava bean purée with sautéed bitter greens. The peasant soul food of Puglia — a side dish that steals the show.
🧁
Pasticciotto
Lecce's custard-filled shortcrust pastry, eaten warm with a caffè for breakfast. Invented here in 1745. Non-negotiable.
🌿
Taralli
Crunchy ring-shaped savory biscuits made with olive oil and wine. Snack constantly. Buy bags to bring home.
🍷
Primitivo & Verdeca
The regional reds (Primitivo — big, dark, extraordinary) and whites (Verdeca — crisp, mineral). Always order local wine.

Good to Know

Practical Notes

🚗
Getting Around
  • Pick up rental car at Bari Airport on Day 1
  • Car essential for Itria Valley — masserias require it
  • Polignano parking limited — use paid lots and walk in
  • Lecce has ZTL zones — confirm hotel parking before driving in
  • Return car at Bari Airport on Day 13
🗓️
Late September Timing
  • August crowds completely gone — meaningfully better
  • Sea still 26–28°C — full swimming season
  • Daytime 26–28°C, evenings 18–20°C — perfect
  • Harvest season — olive oil and wine at peak quality
  • Some beach clubs scale back — book coastal lunches ahead
📋
Book in Advance
  • Grotta Palazzese — book several weeks ahead
  • Tipico Restaurant, Lecce — small and very popular
  • La Pasteria, Martina Franca — fills fast even weeknights
  • Masseria private dinner — arrange at check-in
  • All Lecce hotels — September is still high season
🍽️
Italian Dining Rhythm
  • Lunch: 12:30–2:30pm. Dinner: 8–10pm. Not 6pm.
  • Aperitivo: 6–8pm — Campari, Aperol, local amaro
  • Coperto (cover charge): €2–4/person, completely normal
  • Digestivo (amaro, limoncello): often complimentary
  • Standing espresso at a bar is cheaper and better
🗣️
Language
  • English widely understood in tourist areas
  • Less so in rural Itria Valley — a few phrases go far
  • Google Translate camera mode works perfectly on menus
  • Key words: grazie, prego, per favore, il conto, buongiorno
  • Pugliese dialect is its own world — don't worry about it
💶
Money
  • Cards accepted almost everywhere except street food stalls
  • Bring €20–30 cash per person per day for incidentals
  • Tipping not required; rounding up or €2–5 is appreciated
  • Local wine at dinner: typically €20–40 for a good bottle
  • Masseria rates are higher but worth every cent
